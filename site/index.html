how it works

Three answers. Then it runs.

No levels to set, no dashboard to watch. You tell the desk what matters, it derives the rest from your limits and the market, and keeps adjusting as things move.

1 · what matters most

Protect, balance, or distribute?

Protect keeps the ladder tight under the price and sells slowly. Balanced holds reasonable levels and distributes steadily. Distribute moves supply first and defends lightly, further down.

what the desk does on its own

Runs your book like a desk would, and says why.

Every decision below is made by the engine from rules, not by a chatbot, and reaches you as one line starting with "Desk:". You never have to answer.

defend

Places a ladder under the price

Three trailing levels sit under a one-hour reference, spaced in units of the market's own volatility so noise alone does not hit them, the day's budget weighted to the deeper ones where buying is cheapest.

Desk: Placed 3 defense levels at 0.0001900 (1.25 SOL), 0.0001800 (1.75 SOL), 0.0001650 (2.00 SOL): 5% to 18% under the 1h reference 0.0002000.
breathe

Steps down instead of burning

When holding a level would eat the day's budget in a few hours, or when the last fifteen minutes are one-sided selling, it lets the level breathe and re-places it lower after a cooldown. A level clearly lost is not chased down.

Desk: Holding 0.0001900 costs about 3.8 SOL/hour at this flow and the budget would last 1.3 h. Letting it breathe: stepping the level down.
rest

Stays out of a falling market

Two levels lost in a day and the defense rests for six hours, twelve after three. A 10% drop in five minutes, or a day's mark-to-market loss past the profile's limit, rests it too. It does not buy into a trend with your treasury.

Desk: Level 0.0001720 is lost: price is 16% under it. That is 2 levels lost in 24h, so the defense rests 6 h instead of buying into the trend.
distribute

Sells into real strength, on pace

Leans into strong green candles, backs off when sellers dominate the hour, catches up when behind the horizon, never more than a tenth of the pool's real volume, never past your cap, never a dump at the end.

Desk: Distribution pace 0.30% → 0.15% of the vault per day: sellers dominate this hour, not adding supply to that.
protect

Stops when something is wrong

Pool liquidity gone, a team wallet selling, Tenfold becoming most of the pool's volume, a 35% move in five minutes: it halts, you get the facts, and nothing restarts without you.

Desk: Pool liquidity dropped from about 210 to 48 SOL within the hour, confirmed on several readings. Everything is stopped: trading into a drained pool only loses.
report

Reports every day, in numbers

What sold and at what average against the token's own VWAP, what it cost to defend, where the horizon stands, what you earned in fees. A seven-day version every Monday.

you stay in control

Your money never leaves your reach.

Autonomy only works with hard walls around it. Here is exactly what the desk can and cannot do with your funds.

trade only

It can't withdraw

The desk holds a trading key that can swap and provide liquidity. It cannot send funds anywhere. Withdrawals settle to the wallet you registered at setup, after a delay you chose, and nowhere else.

hard limits

Your limits bind the desk too

The daily sell cap, the defense budget and the floor are checked by the engine on every order the desk produces. Lowering a limit is instant. Raising the sell cap takes 48 hours, so a bad day cannot turn into a bad decision.

your record

Proof you hold

Every deposit, order, fill, decision and withdrawal is logged with a timestamp and a transaction signature. Once a day, a fingerprint of that log is written on-chain. If anyone ever says your project rugged, the full record is yours to show.

what it will not do

The things it says no to are what make the rest believable.

A market maker that will fake volume for you will fake it for the next guy too, and traders know it. Tenfold only does things you could show in daylight.

refused

Wash trading

No buying its own sells, no cycling tokens between wallets, no "making the chart look active". Instead: real distribution into real buy flow, logged in your feed.

refused

Hiding holdings

No splitting the vault across wallets to look smaller, no disguising the team's share. Instead: transparent vesting or a published distribution schedule.

refused

No bots, paid shills, fake holders or fake volume on trackers. Instead: steady liquidity and a trade log you can hand to anyone who asks.

questions

Straight answers.

Can the desk run off with my tokens?+
No. It trades from a wallet you fund, with a key that can only swap and provide liquidity. It cannot withdraw. Funds settle back to the wallet you registered at setup, only after the delay you chose, and you can pause everything from Telegram at any moment.
Do I really not have to watch it?+
You don't. The desk places and moves levels, paces distribution and handles bad hours by itself, and tells you in one line each time. It only asks you for the five things it cannot decide alone: more budget, a cap raise, a liquidity pull, a team wallet selling, the target reached. If you prefer to approve each change, switch it to "ask" mode.
Will it pump my chart?+
No. Tenfold provides real liquidity and sells your supply into real demand. It does not trade against itself or manufacture volume. That is what keeps you clean, and it is also what actually earns you a better average price than dumping.
What happens if the price crashes?+
It defends inside the budget you set and stops chasing once a level is clearly lost. After two lost levels in a day it rests the defense for hours rather than buying into the trend; a 10% drop in five minutes or a day's loss past the profile's limit rests it too, and a 35% move in five minutes holds every order. You get the facts, and the defense comes back on its own when the market calms.
Who can see what it's doing?+
You. Your mandate, levels, sizes, timing and wallet are private, shown only on your own live feed and through your bot. On-chain, a Tenfold trade looks like any other trade. Clip sizes and timing are varied so nobody can front-run the desk.
Can I still take control by hand?+
Yes. Any level you set yourself is pinned: the desk never moves or removes it. Pause is instant. Lowering your cap or budget is instant. Raising the cap takes 48 hours by design. And you can change the mandate at any time in one message.
Can I try it without real money?+
Yes. Every project can start in paper mode: real prices, simulated fills, nothing spent. Switch to live when you are comfortable. In the beta, live vaults start small on purpose.
How do I stop it, or get my funds out?+
Send /pause to your bot and no new order is placed, instantly. To withdraw, request it from the bot: it settles to your registered wallet after the delay you chose at setup, minus the 2% cashout fee. Nobody at Tenfold can redirect it.
Is it audited?+
The beta enforces the cap, the budget and the withdrawal delay in the engine, and keeps vaults small. The on-chain vault program that moves those rules into a contract is being completed and will go through an independent audit before it handles founder funds. We will publish the report.
